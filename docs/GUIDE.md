# The Unstoppable Mind — companion guide

**Version 1.0.0 · PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 · one file, no JavaScript required**

A companion to *The Unstoppable Mind*: the book's key points, its exercises as tickable tasks, a
7-day challenge, flashcards and a toolkit of nine practical moves.

- Live: <https://drsamonline.github.io/unstoppable-mind-companion/play/>
- Offline: download `index.html` and open it in any browser. That single file is the whole app.

## What is inside

| | |
|---|---|
| **The five big ideas** | each chapter with its key points, drawn from the book |
| **Your one move** | the exercise from every chapter, as a tickable task |
| **7-day challenge** | one small move a day to put the ideas to work |
| **12 flashcards** | the book's key terms, tap to flip |
| **Toolkit** | nine practical moves — the five-second move, if-then plans, the three A's and more |

## Why it needs no JavaScript

Tabs, flashcards and checkboxes are pure HTML and CSS. That is not a technical flourish: it means the
app works where scripts are blocked — a locked-down phone, a shared computer, a browser with an
extension doing more than it should. A companion to a book should open in more places than a
framework would allow.

## Your ticks stay yours

Ticks and notes are saved **on your device only**. No accounts, no tracking, nothing leaves the phone.
It also means the browser is the database: clearing site data clears your progress, and the export is
the way to carry it elsewhere.

## Mobile

Add it to your home screen and it opens full-screen, offline. There is nothing to install from a store
and nothing to sign into.
