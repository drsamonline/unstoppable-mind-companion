FAQ

Questions.

Is this the book itself? +

No. It is the companion, not the book. It carries the book's key points and its exercises. The book ties every claim to a named source — for the arguments and the sources, read the book.

Do I need to install anything or sign up? +

No install and no sign-up. It runs in any browser, and it is a single HTML file if you'd rather keep it offline.

Does it work without an internet connection? +

Yes. Open it and it works; nothing is fetched. Once it is loaded (or saved to your device) it keeps working offline.

Do I need JavaScript enabled? +

No. The tabs, flashcards and checkboxes are pure HTML and CSS, so it works even where scripts are blocked.

Where is my data stored? +

On your device only. Your ticks and notes stay in your browser — no accounts, no tracking, nothing leaves your phone.

What does it cost, and can I share it? +

It is free to use. You may use, copy and share it freely with attribution. Please don't sell it or claim you wrote it — see the LICENSE for the exact terms.
